tgradish links a minimal ffmpeg 9.0.2 build, made by `cargo xtask ffmpeg`.
ffmpeg is licensed under the LGPL 2.1 or later. The exact sources of the
build, with how to build tgradish against a modified ffmpeg, are in
ffmpeg-9.0.2-sources.tar, published with each tgradish release at
https://github.com/sliva0/tgradish/releases. They come from:

zlib 1.3.2: https://github.com/madler/zlib/releases/download/v1.3.2/zlib-1.3.2.tar.gz
libvpx 1.17.0: https://github.com/webmproject/libvpx/archive/refs/tags/v1.17.0.tar.gz
dav1d 1.5.4: https://downloads.videolan.org/pub/videolan/dav1d/1.5.4/dav1d-1.5.4.tar.xz
ffmpeg 9.0.2: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
